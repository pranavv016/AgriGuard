About AgriGuard 🌱

A smart, free agricultural information and planning platform built to help farmers identify crop problems, understand their soil, plan output, track farm profit and follow market prices — all in one place.

What is AgriGuard?

AgriGuard is a smart agriculture platform designed to bring together the information farmers need most: crop and flower disease awareness, soil condition guidance, expected harvest output, farm profitability, and market price trends. Instead of visiting several different sources, a farmer can check all of this from a single screen.

The platform is built around real farming decisions. Before sowing, a farmer wants to know if the soil is ready. During the growing season, they need to recognise disease symptoms early. Before harvest, they want to estimate how much produce to expect. After harvest, they need to know whether the season was profitable and what price the market is offering. AgriGuard is organised around each of these stages.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make essential agricultural knowledge simple, accessible and free for every farmer — regardless of farm size, crop type or location. We believe that better information, explained in plain language, leads to better decisions in the field and better outcomes at harvest.

Crop & Flower Disease Awareness

Diseases can reduce both the quality and quantity of a harvest, sometimes sharply. AgriGuard provides educational information about common diseases affecting cereals like wheat and rice, cash crops like cotton and sugarcane, vegetables like tomato and potato, fruit crops like mango, citrus and grapes, and flower crops like rose and marigold.

This information is intended purely for awareness and early recognition. It is not a diagnosis. Farmers should always confirm with a qualified agricultural officer or extension worker before taking any treatment decision.

Soil Health Awareness

Soil pH, moisture and nutrient levels (nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium) strongly influence which crops will thrive. AgriGuard's soil checker turns a few basic readings into a plain-language summary — whether the soil is acidic, alkaline or balanced, whether moisture is adequate, and which crops are typically better suited to that soil type.

Farm Economics — Yield & Profit

Knowing how much a field is likely to produce, and what that produce is likely to be worth, is central to good farm planning. AgriGuard's calculators let a farmer estimate total expected output from their land area, and then work out estimated profit or loss once expenses, yield and selling price are entered.

Market Awareness

Crop and flower prices change with supply, demand, season and local market conditions. AgriGuard presents indicative mandi prices for major crops and flowers in a simple table that refreshes each time the page is used, so farmers can keep an eye on general price movement before deciding when to sell.

Major Crop & Flower Diseases

Learn about common diseases that can affect agricultural crops and flowers.

TOMATO

Early Blight

A common fungal disease affecting tomato and related crops. Dark spots and lesions can develop on leaves.

Warm and humid conditions can encourage disease development.

WHEAT

Wheat Rust

Rust diseases can produce orange-brown or rust-coloured pustules on wheat plants.

Severe infections can affect plant development and grain production.

RICE

Rice Blast

Rice blast is a fungal disease that can produce lesions on leaves and other plant parts.

Severe infections can cause significant yield loss.

POTATO

Late Blight

Late blight can cause dark lesions on leaves and stems of potato and tomato plants.

Cool, wet and humid conditions can favour its spread.

MULTIPLE CROPS

Powdery Mildew

Powdery mildew produces a characteristic white, powder-like growth on plant surfaces.

It can affect many agricultural and horticultural crops, including grapes and mango.

LEGUMES

Downy Mildew

Downy mildew can cause yellow or pale patches on leaves under favourable conditions.

High humidity and prolonged leaf wetness can contribute to development.

🌾
RICE

Bacterial Leaf Blight

A bacterial disease causing water-soaked streaks on leaves that turn yellow and dry, often alongside wilting.

Spreads faster in warm, humid weather and standing water.

🌿
COTTON & BANANA

Fusarium Wilt

A soil-borne fungal disease that blocks water movement inside the plant, causing wilting and yellowing even with enough water.

Infected plants often show soft rot near the base of the stem.

🍊
CITRUS

Citrus Canker

A bacterial disease producing raised, water-soaked spots on leaves, stems and fruit of citrus trees.

Wind-driven rain and insect damage can help it spread between trees.

🥭
MANGO, CHILLI & GRAPES

Anthracnose

A fungal disease causing dark, sunken lesions on leaves, fruit and flowers of several fruit and vegetable crops.

Humid weather during flowering and fruiting increases risk.

🌱
COTTON

Cotton Leaf Curl Virus

A virus spread by whiteflies that causes leaves to curl upward, thicken and show stunted, twisted growth.

Managing whitefly populations helps limit its spread.

🌹
ROSE & MARIGOLD

Botrytis Blight (Grey Mould)

A fungal disease causing grey, fuzzy mould on flower buds, petals and stems, often after cool, damp weather.

Good air circulation and removing spent blooms can reduce spread.
